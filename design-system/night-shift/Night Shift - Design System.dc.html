§1 · NORTH STARS
The table is the product
Everything that is not cards, chips or people is overhead. The system earns its keep by disappearing during a hand.
Money is information
Stacks, pot and the Roll are figures read under time pressure. They line up, they never jitter, they never celebrate at you.
It is play money and it says so
No near-miss theatre, no manufactured urgency, no dark patterns. The honesty is the differentiator.
Legible one-handed, in the dark
Contrast and target size are structural, decided here, not audited later.
Motion reports state
Cards deal, chips travel, the pot moves. Motion carries information about a change and nothing else. A screenshot of the table looks identical to the table at rest.

§5 · CONTROLS
One accented control per screen: the action the clock is waiting on. Everything else is a quiet button. Fold is deliberately the least loud thing on the bar — a product that shouts “fold” is steering, and steering is the thing this brand refuses to do.

§6 · MOTION AND ACCESS
Two durations, one curve
180ms when a thing changes state. 320ms when something travels: a card dealt, chips to the pot. Curve cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1). Nothing loops, nothing pulses, nothing glows to attract. Under reduced motion both collapse to an instant re-set.
The floor
44px targets. Focus visible on every control. Body text and figures at or above 4.5:1 on their own ground, labels above 3:1 and never load-bearing alone. Anything said with colour is also said with a word. The table is operable from the keyboard end to end.
§7 · WHAT THIS SYSTEM REFUSES
Green felt, wood grain, gold anything, drop-shadowed 3D chips, card-flip skeuomorphism, coin-shower wins, countdown urgency that is not a real clock, “nearly!” messaging on a lost hand, and any use of the signal colour that is not the clock waiting on you. Each of these is easy, and each one converts the product from a game into a machine for extracting another hand.
