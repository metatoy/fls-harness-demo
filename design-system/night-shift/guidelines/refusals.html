What this system refuses
  • Green felt, wood grain, gold anything
  • Drop-shadowed 3D chips and card-flip skeuomorphism
  • Coin-shower wins and any celebration that outlasts the information
  • Countdown urgency that is not a real clock
  • “Nearly!” messaging on a lost hand, and near-miss framing of any kind
  • The signal colour used for anything but the clock waiting on you
Each of these is easy, and each one converts the product from a game into a machine for extracting another hand. A refusal list is part of the system because the pressure to add them arrives later, from someone with a number to move.
