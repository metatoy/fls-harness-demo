Two durations, one curve

180ms when a thing changes state. 320ms when something travels: a card dealt, chips to the pot. Curve cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1). Nothing loops, nothing pulses, nothing glows to attract. Under reduced motion both collapse to an instant re-set rather than being removed, because the change is still information.

The floor

44px targets on every control. Focus visible everywhere, in the signal colour. Body text and figures at or above 4.5:1 on their own ground; labels above 3:1 and never load-bearing alone. Anything said with colour is also said with a word. The table is operable from the keyboard end to end.
