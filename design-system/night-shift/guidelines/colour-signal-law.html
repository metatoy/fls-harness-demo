One accent, one meaning
The signal colour means the clock is waiting on you. Win, loss and warning have their own semantic colours precisely so the accent never shares duty.

DO

Your turn Pot won Blinds up

Three states, three colours, three words. A player reading only the words still gets all of it.

DON'T

Your turn Pot won New table

The moment the signal means three things it means nothing, and the player loses the one cue that tells them to act.
